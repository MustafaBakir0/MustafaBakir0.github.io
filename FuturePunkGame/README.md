# Order's End

A text-based political strategy game set in 2050, where you navigate the decline of American global power.

## 🎮 About the Game

Inspired by Ray Dalio's _"Principles for Dealing with a Changing World Order"_, Order's End puts you in the role of the US Representative during a critical moment in history: the dollar is no longer the world's reserve currency, and competing powers are rising.

Every decision you make will shape America's future—and determine the new world order.

## 📊 Core Mechanics

### Stats You Manage

- **⚔️ Military** - National defense capability
- **🎓 Education** - Knowledge, innovation, and human capital
- **💹 Economy** - Economic strength and prosperity
- **🤝 Diplomacy** - International relations and soft power
- **⚖️ Stability** - Internal cohesion and domestic support

### Hidden Systems

- **International Relationships**: Your choices affect relations with China, Russia, EU, and the Global South
- **Decline Momentum**: Poor overall performance makes recovery harder
- **Passive Decline**: Nations in decline face gradual erosion (-1/turn if average < 50%)

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Open `landing.html`** in your browser to start
2. **Read each scene carefully** - context matters for choices
3. **Choose wisely** - every decision has consequences:
   - Cooperation boosts diplomacy and economy, weakens military
   - Sanctions damage economy and diplomacy, boost military
   - Education investment helps long-term, costs short-term
   - Military buildup drains economy and education
4. **Watch your stats** - displayed at the top of the screen
5. **Reach a finale** - all paths lead to one of 32 unique endings

## 🌍 Possible World Orders

Your choices determine which power(s) dominate the global order:

- **USA Restored** - Successfully prevent the fall
- **China Ascendant** - Economic or military Chinese hegemony
- **Bipolar World** - USA-China or USA-Russia competition
- **Multipolar Order** - Power distributed among regions
- **Cooperative Hegemony** - Joint US-China or US-Russia leadership
- **EU/Global South Rise** - New powers emerge

## ⚠️ Difficulty

**This game is intentionally hard.**

The premise is that preventing a superpower's decline is extremely difficult. Most runs will end in decline or fragmented power. Strategic brilliance and calculated sacrifices are required to achieve renewal.

## 🎲 Roguelike Elements

- **Seeded Runs**: Each game has a unique seed for reproducibility
- **Multiple Endings**: 32 distinct finale scenarios
- **Replayability**: Different paths reveal different aspects of geopolitics
- **Run Counter**: Track how many attempts you've made

## 💡 Strategy Tips

1. **Balance is key** - Don't sacrifice everything for one stat
2. **Diplomacy matters** - Good relationships provide options
3. **Short vs Long term** - Some investments take time to pay off
4. **Context awareness** - Read scenes carefully to understand implications
5. **Accept imperfection** - You can't max everything; make trade-offs

## 🏆 Challenge Yourself

- Can you achieve an overall rating above 60%?
- Can you prevent US decline entirely?
- Can you create a truly multipolar democratic order?
- Can you maintain good relationships with all powers?
