About

Order's End is a narrative strategy prototype exploring choices in a shifting global order. Your decisions affect national stats like Military, Education, Economy, Diplomacy, and Stability, leading to different finales. Experiment to find new outcomes.

How to Play

  • Read the scene text to understand the situation.
  • Choose one option; each affects your nation's stats.
  • Watch stats at the top adapt after each decision.
  • Reach a finale; then start a new run to explore other paths.
  • Use Back to review a previous scene if available.
