ORDER'S END

The world order is shifting. Can you prevent the fall of a superpower?

About the Game

It's 2050. The US dollar is no longer the world's reserve currency. The European Euro and Chinese Yuan now hold equal market power. As the country's representative, every decision you make will shape America's future—and the future of the global order itself.

Order's End is a text-based political strategy game inspired by Ray Dalio's "Principles for Dealing with a Changing World Order." Navigate complex diplomatic relationships, manage national resources, and determine whether the United States will rise, fall, or share power in a multipolar world.

🌍 Geopolitical Strategy

Navigate relationships with China, Russia, the EU, and the Global South

📊 Manage Resources

Balance Military, Education, and Economy to prevent national decline

🔀 32 Unique Endings

Every path leads to a different world order—from US renewal to Chinese hegemony
